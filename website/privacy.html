Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

Dishr is a social recipe sharing app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address, display name, and password (stored as a secure hash — we never see your password).
  • Profile information. Optional details you add, such as a bio, avatar photo, and taste preferences from the palate quiz.
  • Content you create. Recipes, photos, cooking logs ("tries"), ratings, comments, collections, messages to other users, and recipes you save or like.
  • Social graph. Who you follow and who follows you.
  • Imported content. If you import a recipe from a URL or social media link, we process that link to extract the recipe.
  • Photos. If you grant permission, we access photos you choose to attach to recipes or tries. We never scan your photo library.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not access your contacts.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

How we use your information

  • To operate the app: show your feed, recipes, profile, and messages.
  • To personalize recipe discovery based on your taste preferences.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g., email verification).

Where your data lives

Dishr is built on Supabase, which hosts our database, authentication, and file storage. Your data is protected by row-level security: other users can only see content you share publicly, and private messages are only visible to participants.

Sharing

Content you post publicly (recipes, tries, comments, your profile) is visible to other Dishr users. We share data with service providers only as needed to run the app (currently Supabase for hosting). We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual recipes and content in the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us at the email below and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Children

Dishr is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Email us and we will honor these requests regardless of your location.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and notify you in the app.
