Cook it. Log it. Share it.

Dishr is a social cookbook. Save recipes from anywhere — websites, TikTok, Instagram — cook them step by step, log every try with photos and ratings, and follow friends to see what's actually worth making.

What you can do

  • Import from anywhere — paste a link or share straight to Dishr and the recipe is extracted for you.
  • Cook mode — full-screen, step-by-step instructions that keep your screen awake.
  • Log your tries — rate every attempt, add notes and photos, and watch your cooking improve.
  • Find by what's in your fridge — search recipes by the ingredients you already have.
  • Follow friends — a feed of real cooking by real people, not food stylists.
